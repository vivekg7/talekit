# Sound credits

Every file in this directory is either dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) by its author or is a
public-domain work of the US federal government. Attribution is not required by
any of them; it is recorded here so the origin of each file stays traceable.
The licence was checked on each file's own page, not on a collection page.
A CC0 recording was still left out when what it plays is someone else's song
(a street flute playing a film tune), and a remix when any of the sounds it
was made from is not CC0 too: the recording's licence does not free either.

Files were trimmed, converted to Opus in Ogg (48 kHz) and level-matched:
sound effects are mono and peak-normalised to about -1 dBFS; ambience beds are
stereo, cut at quiet moments with no fades (the mixer loops and crossfades them)
and normalised to about -23 LUFS; music is stereo at about -18 LUFS.
